# Problem Statement

## Background

Ayurvedic formulation knowledge can be difficult to navigate because information may be distributed across traditional terminology, modern descriptions, structured records, and research references.

## Core challenge

The challenge is to convert a user's natural-language intent into a reliable set of structured Ayurvedic concepts and then retrieve and explain relevant formulations without hiding uncertainty or provenance.

## Technical gap

A simple keyword search can miss:
- synonyms
- alternate spellings
- traditional terminology
- semantic relationships
- ingredient/formulation relationships
- evidence context

## Proposed technical response

The platform combines terminology normalization, hybrid retrieval, ranking, structured knowledge, and evidence-aware presentation.
