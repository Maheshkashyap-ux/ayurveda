# Safety and Responsible Use

## Positioning

The platform is an information and research-support prototype. It must not claim to diagnose disease, prescribe treatment, or replace professional judgment.

## Safety mechanisms

- Clear scope statement.
- Visible warnings where appropriate.
- Provenance for important claims.
- Graceful handling of uncertainty.
- No fabricated evidence.
- No hidden safety filtering.
- Clear distinction between ranking and clinical efficacy.

## Sensitive inputs

If a query indicates a situation outside the supported scope, the system should provide a safe limitation message rather than forcing a recommendation.

## Demo language

Use phrases such as:
- "information retrieved from the knowledge base"
- "ranked candidate"
- "supporting reference"
- "prototype result"

Avoid presenting the output as a confirmed diagnosis or prescription.
