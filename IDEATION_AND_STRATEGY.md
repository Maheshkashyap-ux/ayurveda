# Ideation and Strategy

## 1. Problem

Ayurvedic information is distributed across traditional references, structured datasets, research literature, and modern terminology. A user may describe a need in everyday language while relevant information is stored under traditional or alternate terminology.

## 2. Proposed solution

Build a searchable and explainable intelligence layer that connects:

- user intent
- normalized terminology
- Ayurvedic entities
- formulations
- ingredient relationships
- evidence / references
- safety information

## 3. Differentiation

The project is not positioned as a generic chatbot. Its value comes from structured knowledge, retrieval, provenance, explainability, and safety-aware presentation.

## 4. Hackathon strategy

The demonstration should prioritize one complete vertical slice:

```text
User query
   ↓
Normalization
   ↓
Relevant entities
   ↓
Candidate formulations
   ↓
Ranking
   ↓
Evidence + explanation
   ↓
Safety-aware result
```

A smaller working flow is stronger than many disconnected features.

## 5. Success criteria

A judge should be able to understand:

1. The problem.
2. Why existing search is insufficient.
3. How the platform transforms the query.
4. How candidates are retrieved.
5. Why a result is ranked.
6. What evidence supports the result.
7. What safety limitations are applied.
